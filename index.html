About Hanyi

涵
一

Hanyi Zhou is a young poet from Hong Kong. Her work explores the intersections of memory, displacement, and the quiet beauty hidden in everyday moments — from the steam rising off a cup of pu-erh to the hum of a city holding its breath.

She is always collecting small stories from the people she meets and finds joy in all forms of literature. She enjoys cooking and would love to hear about your favorite family recipe.
